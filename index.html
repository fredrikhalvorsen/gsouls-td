Game Info

Damage Upgrade Bonus

Each level of the Damage upgrade increases tower damage by +35% of base damage per level.

Starting at level 100, every 25 levels gives an additional +5% bonus on top of total damage.

Example: Level 150 = 2 bonus tiers = +10% extra damage.

Every 1000 levels gives an additional x1.25 damage multiplier (stacks multiplicatively).

Example: Level 3000 = 3 milestones = x1.95 total multiplier.

Gold Mine

Base gold per tick: 3. Each Gold Amt level adds +20% of base.

Gold income scales with your highest wave this reset: +1% per wave cleared.

Example: At wave 50, your Gold Mine earns 50% more gold.

Tick speed starts at 5s and decreases with Speed upgrades (min 0.2s).

Gsouls (Endless Mode)

Gsouls start dropping from bosses at wave 50.

Each boss has a 40% chance to drop Gsouls.

Drop amount: 1 + floor(wave / 25) per drop.

Examples: Wave 50 = 1-2, Wave 75 = 1-3, Wave 100 = 1-4.

Each collected Gsoul gives +10% damage to all towers.

Use Reset for Gsouls to collect pending Gsouls. This resets your wave to 1, removes all towers and gold, but keeps your Gsouls and tower cap.

Tower Cap

You start with 5 tower slots. Every 50 waves reached (across all resets) grants +1 slot permanently.

Cluster Waves

Every 5th wave (starting wave 3) spawns clusters of weaker enemies (-30% HP).

Cluster size: Waves 1-20: 3x, 21-40: 4x, 41-60: 5x, 61+: 7x.
